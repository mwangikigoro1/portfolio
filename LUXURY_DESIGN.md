## 🎯 Premium Animations

### Shine Effect
Buttons and links have a horizontal shine that travels across on hover:
```css
::before gradient animation (left: -100% → 100%)
```

### Glow Effects
- Gold shadows at 0.15-0.3 opacity
- Creates soft, elegant depth
- Never harsh or overwhelming

### Smooth Transitions
- All transitions use: `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)`
- Premium easing function
- 0.3s duration for sophistication

### Scale Animations
- Cards elevate on hover (translateY -5px to -8px)
- Buttons scale smoothly
- Social icons scale 1.1x with elevation
